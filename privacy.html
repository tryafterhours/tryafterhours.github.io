afterhours Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Version: 2026-10-draft

The short version

  • We're built so nobody sees you unless you both choose each other. There's no browsing, no public profile and no feed.
  • Your birthday never leaves your phone. We only learn that you're 18 or over.
  • Your verification selfie is never shown to members, never used to train AI, and deleted when you delete your account.
  • We never post to LinkedIn, message your contacts, or contact your employer.
  • You can download or delete your data at any time. Deletion is finished within 72 hours.
  • We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

afterhours ("we", "us") is based in Bengaluru, India. We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), and we also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

Contact: privacy@tryafterhours.app · Grievance Officer: see section 11.

2. Who this applies to

This policy covers anyone who uses the afterhours app or website. The app is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we delete it.

4. How matching and AI work

  • Matching: when you send a Spark, our system checks it against verified members at that company. Your Spark is sent to our matching model only if at least one verified member could match. A match is created only when both people have Sparked each other.
  • Anonymity, and its limits: we never tell anyone who Sparked them unless it's mutual. Notifications are grouped and delayed, and numbers are shown in ranges, so they can't be used to guess who's on the app. But we can't stop people guessing. In a small team, someone may work it out. Please Spark kindly.
  • Notes on Priority Sparks are checked by an automated safety filter before delivery.
  • Verification: every member is verified before they can use afterhours.
  • We do not use your messages, selfie or photos to train AI models.

5. Legal basis

We process your data with your consent (DPDP Act, s. 6), which you give during onboarding. We also rely on legitimate uses (s. 7), such as complying with law, court orders or a medical emergency. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 8). Withdrawing means we can no longer provide the service, so your account will be deleted.

8. Your rights (DPDP Act)

You can: - access a summary of your data and download it (Settings → Your data); - correct your details. Workplace changes need re-verification. Your name comes from LinkedIn and can't be edited; - delete your account (Settings → Delete account). It's deactivated at once and erased within 72 hours; - withdraw consent, which is the same as deleting your account; - nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to; - complain to our Grievance Officer (section 11), then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Your data export includes your profile, your own Sparks, your purchases and your consents. It never includes other people's data.

9. Security

We protect your data with industry-standard safeguards. That includes encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls so members can only reach their own data, two-step sign-in for our team, logging of every access to verification selfies, and secure storage of sign-in details on your phone. Your data is stored in India. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app and ask for consent again where the law requires it. The version you agreed to is recorded with your account.

11. Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, afterhours · grievance@tryafterhours.app

We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days. That is faster than the legal maximum.
