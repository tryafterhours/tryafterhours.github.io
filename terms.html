1. Agreement

These terms are an agreement between you and afterhours ("afterhours", "we"), based in Bengaluru, India. They apply when you create an account, which needs your explicit acceptance. Our Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines are part of these terms.

2. Who can join

You must: 1. be at least 18 years old; 2. sign in with your own LinkedIn account. One person = one account; 3. work at the company you name, and have the right to say so; 4. not be banned from afterhours, and not be legally barred from using it; 5. not have been convicted of a sexual or violent offence.

3. Verification

Every member is verified before they can use afterhours. Verification confirms you're a real person at the workplace you name. It isn't a background check, so always meet in public and trust your instincts.

4. Using afterhours

  • You can privately show interest in someone (a Spark). They only find out it was you if they Spark you too.
  • We never reveal who Sparked someone, but we can't stop people from guessing.
  • Some features have limits, like how many Sparks you can have or how long a chat stays open. We always show these in the app.
  • We may change, add or remove features as afterhours grows.

5. Your conduct

You agree to follow the Community Guidelines. In particular, you won't: - Spark or message anyone in your reporting line, whether direct or indirect; - harass, threaten, pressure, stalk, or keep contacting someone who has said no; - send sexual content without consent, or any content involving minors; - screenshot, record, share or publish anything from afterhours, or use what you learn here off the app (including at work); - try to find out who is on afterhours. For example: sending Sparks to test it, scraping, making fake accounts or coordinating with others; - impersonate anyone, lie about your workplace, or use someone else's LinkedIn or photos; - sell anything, advertise, recruit, or ask for money; - reverse-engineer, scrape, overload or attack the app.

  • A ban means your LinkedIn account can't be used to sign up again.
  • Appeals: email grievance@tryafterhours.app within 30 days and we'll review it again.
  • Workplace harassment: afterhours is not a substitute for your employer's Internal Committee (ICC) under the POSH Act, 2013.
